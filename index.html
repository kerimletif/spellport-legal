Spellport — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Spellport ("the App", package name com.spellport.game) handles information. The short version: Spellport does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

1. Data We Collect

None. The App does not collect any personal information, usage data, device identifiers, location data, or analytics of any kind. The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your game progress (current level, score, found words, collected cities, and saved board state) is stored only locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes it.

3. Internet Access

The App works fully offline and makes no network requests.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no data at all, it does not collect data from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

5. Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the App adds features that involve data collection (for example, advertising or analytics), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: mr.letif.kerim@gmail.com
