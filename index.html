Spellport: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spellport ("the App", package name com.spellport.game) handles information. The short version: the App has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. It shows optional video ads from Google AdMob, only when you tap a button to watch one for a reward. To deliver those ads, Google collects some device data, as described in section 3.

1. Who we are

Spellport is made by an independent developer. You can reach us at the email address in section 12.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, scores, coins, found words, collected cities, the saved board, hint counts and sound settings) is stored only on your device. We cannot see or access it. The App sends nothing to us, and we run no servers. Uninstalling the App deletes it permanently.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. An ad plays only after you tap a button that names the reward, for example an extra life, a letter or a hint. You can always say no, and the game works without watching any ad.

When the App requests or shows an ad, Google may collect and process:

  • the advertising identifier of your device (the Android Advertising ID; on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking, see section 5);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and App version;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and tapped;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information from the ads SDK.

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, where the law and your choices allow it, to personalize ads. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. We only see summary reports, such as how many ads were shown. Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and on the page How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

Where the law requires it, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before any ad is requested. To change your choice later, clear the App's data (Android) or reinstall the App (iOS). The consent message will then appear again.

5. Tracking on iOS

On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) can only be used if you allow it in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow it, you still get ads, but they are not personalized with the IDFA. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

6. Your choices

  • Ads are optional: simply do not tap the video buttons.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).
  • iOS: you can turn tracking off as described in section 5.

7. Internet access

The game itself plays offline. The App uses the internet only to load ads and the consent message.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, contact us and we will help you request its deletion from Google.

9. Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies. Resetting your advertising ID breaks the link between your device and the data collected before.

10. International transfers

Google may process ad data on servers outside your country. Google describes its safeguards in its privacy policy.

11. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: crawongames@gmail.com
